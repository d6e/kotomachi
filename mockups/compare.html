ことまち: pick an art style

The same street and the same cafe conversation in four takes. Everything is original pixel art drawn in code, at 3x scale. The dialogue text is real text, so furigana and word lookups keep working.

A: Classic handheldB: Soft pastelC: Warm and detailedD: Later handheld, full colour

AClassic handheld

Four shades of green, chunky outlines and a double-ruled text box, like the first pocket consoles.

BSoft pastel

A limited set of nine soft colours with plum outlines and a rounded box: the cutest and calmest of the four.

CWarm and detailed

About ten warm colours with a little more detail (tiled roofs, brick paving, a wooden text frame).

DLater handheld, full colour

Full colour with 2 to 3 step shading, drop shadows, flower boxes and shop posters, and a rounded blue box.
